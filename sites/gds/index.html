I listen to GDS.FM while I work.

You probably should too. So I built this little badge that shows what's spinning right now. Drop it in your email signature, your README, your bio — wherever images render. Every time someone opens it, the track updates.

Live. Refresh and the track changes.

GDS.FM is volunteer-run, ad-free, and the best radio in Zürich. Live shows Wednesday to Saturday from the bar-and-studio on Anwandstrasse, plus handpicked setblocks the rest of the time. If you also love it: become a member. If you can't, share the badge with someone who'd dig it.

Or fork it

The whole thing is one tiny Cloudflare Worker. Point it at any radio station's now-playing API and you have a live badge for any station. The code is on GitHub. MIT license. Take it, make one for your favorite station.
